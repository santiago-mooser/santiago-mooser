Round 02 / More character, more possibilities

Four different ways
to make it yours.

A personal magazine. A photographic journal. An illustrated cabinet. A colourful space after dark. Different ways to give your writing a home.

01

The Sunday Edition

Editorial · Layered · Assured

A generous masthead, a proper lead story, harbour photography and thoughts in the margins. Your own small magazine, with slate ink and vermilion accents.

My shortlist: 01 or 03.

The Sunday Edition gives your writing presence and keeps the strongest connection to your current palette. The Curiosity Cabinet has the most personal, exploratory character.

All four include a reading specimen and working topic filters. Imagery can be optional per post; you should never have to design a cover just to write something.

Built around your words.

The proposed workflow remains Markdown → static HTML → GitHub Pages. The visual direction does not require a CMS server or a different way of writing.
