On leaving room for things to happen

Design sample: this is illustrative copy, not a published post or a statement of Santiago’s views. All sample post links lead here.

There is a certain comfort in having a plan. A list turns the unknown into something that fits on a page. It gives the day a shape before the day has begun.

But lately I have been wondering about the things that never make it onto the list. The longer way home. A conversation that wanders. An afternoon without much to show for it.

A little space around the edges

This is where an idea can take its time. There is no need for every observation to become an argument, or every interest to become a project.

Perhaps a personal website can be a place to notice things before deciding what they mean.

A paragraph, a photograph, a small discovery. Enough to remember it by. Enough to leave the door open for another thought.

Room for technical notes, too

The same reading layout can accommodate code without making the whole site feel like documentation.
